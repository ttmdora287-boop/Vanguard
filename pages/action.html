Year 9 PBL Project Earthquake Risk Reduction

Community Action & Disaster Support

Since our core mission is reducing seismic risk, taking proactive steps before the next major earthquake is vital. Support our pre-disaster relief campaigns, coordinate item pickups if you are busy, or join our educational conferences and podcasts to build a resilient Mandalay.

Pre-Disaster Relief

Pre-Earthquake Donation Campaign

Help us stockpile emergency water, medical first-aid kits, and go-bag reserves before the next big disaster strikes. Contributions ensure our community stadiums and families are fully equipped ahead of time.

  • Support financial funds for structural safety reinforcement.
  • Donate physical items (flashlights, whistles, canned food, clean water).
  • Receive an official personalized Vanguard Philanthropist Certificate instantly upon completion!
Empowering Resilience in Advance

Your timely contributions before an emergency happen create an unbreakable safety net for schools, orphanages, and neighborhoods across Mandalay.

Flexible Community Support

Item Pickup & Away Volunteering

Are you busy, unable to travel, or located far away? No problem! Our student volunteer dispatch team can arrange a home or office pickup for your relief items, or you can register to assist remotely with awareness coordination before the next earthquake.

Thank you! Our Vanguard dispatch team will contact you soon to arrange item pickup.
No Distance is Too Far to Help

Even if you are busy or reside outside central downtown, your donated supplies are collected by our student dispatchers and pre-positioned in secure local staging areas.

Education & Outreach

Conferences, Podcasts & School Presentations

Knowledge is our strongest shield. We host interactive conferences, expert podcast episodes with seismic geologists, and safety workshops specifically tailored for school children and local communities before the next disaster strikes.

Registration successful! We will email you the conference link or schedule details.
Spreading Awareness to Vulnerable Groups

Our student team regularly presents live safety demonstrations, distributing whistles and survival guides to children and teachers across Mandalay.
