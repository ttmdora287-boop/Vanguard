Frequently Asked Questions

Expert answers regarding earthquake preparation, disaster response, and safety protocols.

Execute the Drop, Cover, and Hold On protocol immediately. Drop to your hands and knees, crawl under a sturdy table or desk for shelter, and hold onto it until all shaking completely ceases. Avoid running outdoors during active tremors.

Your go-bag should contain at least 72 hours (3 days) of self-sufficiency supplies, including 1 gallon of drinking water per person daily, non-perishable food items, first-aid supplies, flashlights, and vital personal documents.

Soil liquefaction happens when saturated, loose soil temporarily loses its solid strength during heavy ground shaking and behaves like thick quicksand, causing heavy buildings and infrastructure to sink or tip over.

Cellular networks easily get jammed immediately after major earthquakes due to sudden call volume spikes. Use text messaging (SMS) or internet data apps instead to preserve bandwidth for emergency responders.
